## Model tiers

A tier is named, never a model version. `nen/workflow.json` → `models` maps four tiers to one alias per surface, and a role to a tier; the live id is read from the surface when a skill runs, never remembered. A subagent never runs on the frontier tier, which is where your own conversation lives.

## CI tiers

An adopted repository can also carry CI reviewer lanes, installed by `kuchiyose` (install a CI tier) as one version-controlled PR: it reads the tier catalogue at the pinned provider tag, asks the per-repository rulings, writes the caller workflow and the gate entries, and prints the day-one ceremony that stays yours (the Apps, the secrets, the required-checks ruleset). It never creates an App, sets a secret, applies a ruleset or merges. Hosted, autonomous runs of the whole loop are the future Cloud tier, coming later.
